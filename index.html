RESEARCH DOMAIN · R26-IT-127

Intelligent Railway Operations for Sri Lanka

Our proposed research explores how artificial intelligence, real-time data and optimization can support safer, more reliable and passenger-focused railway operations.

THE RESEARCH VISION

From reactive railway management to intelligent decision support.

Traditional railway operations face challenges with manual ticket inspections, limited real-time train information, unpredictable delays, fluctuating passenger demand and static capacity planning. Ceylon Railway investigates four complementary research components designed to turn railway data into actionable predictions and operational recommendations.

Ticket SecurityLive TrackingSmart SchedulingDemand & Capacity
BACKGROUND & CHALLENGES

Understanding the research problem

Our proposal studies identify several connected challenges in contemporary railway operations.

🎫

Manual ticket inspection

Random checks can miss abnormal ticket reuse, concession misuse and suspicious resale behavior.

📍

Uncertain train arrivals

Static timetables and limited real-time visibility make unexpected train delays difficult to anticipate.

🚉

Uneven passenger demand

Peak-hour overcrowding and off-peak underutilization reduce passenger comfort and operational efficiency.

LITERATURE SURVEY & RESEARCH GAP

Why an integrated approach matters

EXISTING APPROACHES

What prior research explores

Published work covers secure digital ticket validation, behavioral fraud detection, GPS/IoT monitoring, deterministic train arrival estimation, classical demand forecasting and independent resource optimization.

IDENTIFIED LIMITATIONS

Where the gaps remain

Important gaps include explainable fraud risk and inspection workload, real-time probabilistic delay estimation, spatial relationships in station demand, and the limited integration of forecasts with adaptive seat allocation.
